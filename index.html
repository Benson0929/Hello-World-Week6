Time, translated into geometry.

Moiré Time is a generative clock that transforms real time into continuously evolving geometric patterns through four mathematical systems. Built with p5.js, it maps hours, minutes, and seconds to different visual parameters, exploring how time can be experienced beyond numbers and clock hands.
